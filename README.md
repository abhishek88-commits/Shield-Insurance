# Shield Insurance – Sales and Revenue Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

An end-to-end Power BI business analysis project developed as part of the Codebasics Virtual Internship. This project analyzes Shield Insurance's business performance over a six-month period (November 2022 – April 2023), focusing on revenue trends, geographical performance, sales channel effectiveness, customer segmentation, policy preferences, and expected settlements.

### Key Highlights
- **Overall Performance:** Analyzed approximately ₹989M in revenue and 26,841 customers.
- **Revenue Trends:** Identified a significant revenue spike in March, potentially influenced by tax-season activity.
- **Geographical Analysis:** Identified Delhi NCR as the highest contributor to revenue and customer acquisition, followed by Mumbai.
- **Sales Mode Analysis:** Compared Offline Agent, Offline Direct, Online App, and Online Website channels. Offline Agent emerged as the leading channel, while online policy purchases showed increasing adoption.
- **Customer Segmentation:** Identified the 30–39 age group as the highest contributor to revenue and customer volume.
- **Policy Preference Analysis:** Evaluated differences in policy coverage preferences across age groups.
- **Expected Settlement Analysis:** Explored expected settlements across customer age groups.

### Dashboard Pages
1. **General View:** Overall KPIs, monthly revenue trends, geographical contribution, and customer segmentation.
2. **Sales Mode Analysis:** Customer and revenue contribution across sales channels, including monthly trends.
3. **Age Group Analysis:** Age-wise policy preferences, sales mode distribution, customer trends, and expected settlements.
